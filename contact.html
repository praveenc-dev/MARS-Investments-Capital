FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions we hear most from new investors.

Is my money safe with Mars Investments?+

Yes, absolutely. Mars Investments Capital is a SEBI-registered Investment Advisor. Your funds are held in your own demat account linked to NSE/BSE — we never hold client money directly. Your investments are protected under SEBI and CDSL/NSDL regulations.

What is the minimum investment amount to get started?+

You can start investing in mutual funds with as little as ₹500 per month via SIP. For equity trading, there's no minimum — you can buy even 1 share. Fixed deposits and bonds start at ₹1,000. PMS requires a minimum investment of ₹50 lakhs.

Are there any hidden charges or fees?+

No hidden fees — ever. Account opening is free. Brokerage for the first 30 days is zero. After that, our transaction fees are some of the lowest in the industry, clearly listed on our pricing page. We believe in 100% transparent pricing.

How long does it take to open an account?+

Account opening is completely digital and takes 10-15 minutes. You'll need your PAN card, Aadhaar card, and bank account details. Once submitted, your account is typically activated within 24-48 hours.

Can I talk to a human advisor instead of using the app?+

Absolutely! Every client gets access to a dedicated human advisor you can call or WhatsApp during business hours (Mon-Sat, 9am-6pm). Growth and Wealth plan subscribers get priority access with faster response times.

How do I withdraw my money when I need it?+

Withdrawing funds is simple. For equity, funds are credited to your bank account within T+1 trading day. For mutual funds, redemption takes 1-3 working days depending on the fund type. FD withdrawals depend on the tenure chosen.
