Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Results

Mars Investments Capital was founded in 2009 with a simple but powerful vision — to make world-class investment guidance accessible to every Indian, regardless of their financial background or experience level.

What started as a small advisory firm in Chennai has grown into a pan-India financial powerhouse with 500+ branches, 10 lakh+ clients, and ₹500 crore+ in assets under management.

Today, we are proud to be one of India's most trusted SEBI-registered investment advisors — offering everything from equity trading and mutual funds to US stocks and portfolio management services.
